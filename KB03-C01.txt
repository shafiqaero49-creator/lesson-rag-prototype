Title: USGS plate-boundary teaching resource
Source: USGS Educational Resources

The three main plate-boundary interactions are divergent, convergent, and transform. At transform boundaries plates slide past one another and crust is neither created nor destroyed. A paper model of the San Andreas Fault system can help learners model relative movement of the Pacific and North American plates. The USGS resource is intended for grades 4-12.
